01 — About

Two disciplines, one way of thinking.

I'm currently pursuing my B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering at Pondicherry University, based out of Kalapet while home is Asansol, West Bengal.

Most of my week is split between two very different rhythms — writing and reasoning through code, and slowing down with a paintbrush or a sheet of paper. I've found the two aren't as far apart as they seem: both reward patience, structure, and an eye for what's slightly off.

Academically that shows up as consistency — a 9.56 CGPA and school ranks that didn't happen by accident. Outside the classroom, it shows up as ideathons, competitions, and a growing stack of half-finished paintings.
